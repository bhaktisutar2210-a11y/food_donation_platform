About Our Platform

FoodSave is an intelligent food waste reduction and donation platform that helps donors connect with NGOs and receivers to reduce food wastage.

How It Works

1. Donate

Donors add information about surplus food.

2. Connect

NGOs and receivers find available food.

3. Collect

Food is collected and delivered to people in need.
